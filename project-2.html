Smart Banking: Landing Page

Landing page with pricing and FAQ

RoleProduct / UI-UX Designer
ToolsFigma

The problem

A landing page that tells visitors what they get from the banking service before they sign up: one card for all payments, quick money management, dependable customer support, fair pricing, and a free plan alongside premium.

Key decision

Pricing sits on the landing page on purpose. Nobody should sign up and only later discover a premium tier. Free and premium are clear from the first scroll.

What I changed

The plan cards. Free and premium use two different colors, so the difference is obvious at a glance.

What I would change today

Run proper user research first, then rebuild the FAQ around the questions people actually ask.

Beyond the hero

I also designed the pricing and FAQ sections, so visitors can learn about plans and ask questions before committing.
