SELF-INITIATED CONCEPT

Fintech Mobile App: Home & Navigation

Fintech app for daily spending

RoleProduct / UI-UX Designer
ToolsFigma

The problem

Helping people manage their daily spending. Once users have an account, they can see the money coming in and going out, and set a monthly limit on what leaves their account or card, so they always know where they stand against their budget.

Why these screens

Simplicity. The dashboard and navigation are built so nobody has to hunt for a hidden icon. Open the navigation and everything they need is right there.

Key decision

The dashboard opens with a chart of money in versus money out, followed by recent transactions. The moment someone opens the app, they see how their money is moving without digging.

What I weighed

Whether the balance card should show the amount left or the amount spent. I kept it as an up-to-date view of where the user stands in their budget, so a quick glance is enough.

What I would change today

Make it simpler still, and add a dedicated Transactions icon to the bottom navigation so users reach their transactions in one tap.

The full flow

I designed the whole app myself, from onboarding and sign in to OTP verification and confirmation.
