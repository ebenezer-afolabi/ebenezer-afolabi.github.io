WHO I AM

I'm Ebenezer Odunayo Afolabi, a Product Designer and Creative Designer focused on turning ideas into intuitive digital products and compelling visual experiences.

My work sits at the intersection of UI/UX, visual design, and emerging AI technology, combining creative thinking with purposeful design to solve problems and communicate ideas clearly.

With a foundation in graphic and visual design, coupled with a deep understanding of design principles, I care about creating digital experiences that are simple, functional, visually engaging, and meaningful to users.

My practice in Product Design comes through real projects: interface design, interaction design, wireframing, prototyping, and continuous exploration. I'm open to Product Design opportunities, freelance projects, and creative collaborations.

Creative Approach

How a project moves from an open question to a finished interface.

01

Research & Analysis

Understanding the user and the problem before touching a screen: what people need, and what the product is actually trying to solve.

02

Define & Ideate

Turning that understanding into concepts through wireframes, user flows, and rounds of iteration.

03

Execution & Delivery

Building high-fidelity screens and prototypes that hold up under real use, not just in a static mockup.

04

Review & Refine

Stepping back, testing assumptions, and tightening details until the interface feels obvious to use.
